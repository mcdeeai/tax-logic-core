## Summary

- **214 ledger rows:** 90 CORRECT, 41 WRONG, 80 MISAPPLIED, 2 MISSING-GATE, 1 STALE-COMMENT, and 0 UNVERIFIED. All 1,323 lines were read. No files were modified.
- **Material rate errors:** NJ, GA, MD, WI, NC, OR, and SC. NY’s encoded brackets are correct, but the calculation applies a marginal rate to the entire income amount.
- **Election errors:** CT is incorrectly marked mandatory; CA, GA, IL, MN, and WI have incorrect election instructions. NY, NJ, and MN estimated-payment dates also contain errors.
- **Federal savings are overstated or suppressed incorrectly:** both $40,000 caps ignore `useObbba2025`, filing status, and the MAGI phase-down. Savings omit changes to itemized deductions, QBI, brackets, payment timing, and state tax.
- **Other material errors:** California’s married-separate surtax threshold is **$1 million**, not $500,000; California does not recognize QOZ deferral; Texas’s $1.23 million threshold and No Tax Due Report instructions are obsolete.
- **Qualifying PTET deductions remain outside the individual SALT cap in 2025.** Enacted OBBBA does not impose the proposed PTET deduction restrictions. State-law values generally remain the same under both federal policy-flag settings.

## Open questions

- **Input contract:** Are `scheduleK1.ordinaryIncome`, `sCorpIncome`, and `partnershipIncome` distinct income streams or overlapping representations? Does each amount describe an owner’s share or the entire entity? The graduated calculations require entity-level information.
- **Election facts:** Where are federal/state entity classifications, owner residency/type, consent, nexus, actual elections, and actual payment dates represented? This file does not establish those facts.
- **Savings definition:** Should `potentialSavings` represent federal-only savings or net federal-and-state savings? The descriptions presently imply both.
- **Texas period:** Does “tax year 2025” mean the **2025 franchise report**, or a calendar-2025 accounting period reported in **2026**? The latter uses the 2026 report’s $2.65 million threshold and $480,000 compensation limit.
- **Modeling heuristics:** Are the $50,000 screening threshold, 50% COGS assumption, 30% compensation assumption, and hypothetical $100,000/$50,000 income reductions intentional product assumptions? They should not be presented as statutory limits or substantiated available savings.
- **Source versioning:** NJ retains an official page displaying obsolete BAIT brackets, while its dated 2025 PTE-100 instructions show the corrected three-tier schedule. Current state websites also contain later-year amendments. A human should decide the engine’s source-version policy; the ledger uses provisions applicable to 2025.
