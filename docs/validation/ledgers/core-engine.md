## Open questions

- **Are monetary fields raw inputs or completed tax-form amounts?** `hsaDeduction`, `iraDeduction` and retirement deductions imply precomputed amounts; `studentLoanInterest`, charitable gifts, casualty losses and several credit fields are less clear. Upstream validation could resolve some conditional findings, but it cannot cure the explicit computation errors.
- **What exactly does K-1 ordinary income include?** If guaranteed payments are already excluded, subtracting them again understates QBI. Separately entered partnership/S-corporation income affects QBI but does not enter total income unless also supplied through Schedule E or another income field.
- **Can the input represent each spouse separately?** Correct joint-return SE calculations require ownership of profits and SS wages; senior deductions require both spouses’ birth dates and eligibility.
- **Do tips and overtime already appear in wages/business income?** The deduction fields are not themselves added to income. Their qualified portion must be derived under 2025 guidance without omitting taxable receipts or counting them twice.
- **Is this an estimator or a filed-return-equivalent engine?** Tax Table differences, rounding, omitted forms, and unsupported special cases need an explicit product boundary. There is no unresolved primary-source conflict on the six reviewer hypotheses.
