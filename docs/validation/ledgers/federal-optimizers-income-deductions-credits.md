## Open questions

- **Input contracts:** Are mortgage interest, charitable gifts, casualty losses and actual vehicle expenses raw expenditures or already legally limited deduction amounts? Several findings are conditional on raw inputs. Medical expenses are demonstrably treated as raw expenses by the engine, while optimizer totals treat them as deductible amounts.
- **Retirement fields:** Does `retirement401k` contain only pretax deferrals, or also Roth deferrals? Are spouse contributions, employer HSA contributions, plan coverage and actual compensation available elsewhere? These distinctions are necessary to determine remaining room.
- **403(b) coverage:** There is no separate 403(b) analyzer. Ordinary 2025 limits match the verified $23,500/$7,500/$11,250 figures, but eligible 15-year-service catch-up rules require separate treatment. Decide whether this optimizer promises that coverage.
- **SIMPLE/solo coverage:** SIMPLE lacks its own calculation, and solo 401(k) constants are unused. Decide whether these are intended supported optimization paths; a shared SEP/SIMPLE deduction field cannot establish contribution room.
- **Taxpayer facts:** Acquisition/service dates, tip occupation and reporting, FLSA-qualified premium, vehicle loan/VIN facts, qualifying care/student facts, and spouse allocations are missing. The laws are generally clear; recommendations need those facts.
- **2026 projections:** Charitable bunching explicitly reaches the next year, but uses a 2025-only estimate. A genuine 2025/2026 comparison needs 2026 charitable rules and standard deductions. Likewise, OBBBA’s wider QBI ranges and dependent-care expansion belong to 2026.
- **Crypto classification:** The unconditional wash-sale statement needs a defined asset scope. The statute supports treatment based on whether an asset is stock or securities, not its “crypto” label.
- **Recommendation versus implemented scenario:** Decide whether savings are merely illustrative screens or promised executable outcomes. Current output calls a sum of overlapping estimates “optimized tax,” suppresses zero-savings informational strategies, and cannot apply most selected recommendations.
