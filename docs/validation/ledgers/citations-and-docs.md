## Summary

- 52 ledger rows: **21 CORRECT, 4 WRONG, 13 MISAPPLIED, 11 MISSING-GATE, 2 STALE-COMMENT, 1 UNVERIFIED**.
- The clearest numeric errors are the 2025 AMT phaseout thresholds and the SEP/Solo 401(k) annual contribution ceiling. The 2025 defined-contribution-plan limit is **$70,000**, not $69,000.
- Several OBBBA descriptions omit material limits or eligibility rules. The new deductions’ forms and the senior deduction’s legal citation/placement are also inaccurate.
- README calls pre-OBBBA assumptions a “current-law fallback.” OBBBA was enacted July 4, 2025, and its relevant amendments generally apply to 2025; pre-OBBBA figures are a comparison scenario.
- `taxAuthority.js` is static metadata with no `useObbba2025` branch. I mark OBBBA entries there MISSING-GATE because they display OBBBA values without distinguishing the requested policy setting; this does not establish how calculations outside the requested scope behave.
