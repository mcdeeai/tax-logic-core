## Summary

- **398 ledger rows:** 182 CORRECT, 79 WRONG, 129 MISAPPLIED, 2 MISSING-GATE, 6 STALE-COMMENT, 0 UNVERIFIED. A row can contain several related defects; counts are ledger rows, not individual bugs.
- **28 of 51 state/DC rate-system rows have errors.** Material examples include Iowa’s obsolete graduated brackets, Louisiana’s obsolete graduated brackets, outdated California and Hawaii schedules, and missing Maryland brackets.
- **Credit calculations can substantially overstate or omit benefits.** California’s EITC helper returns the maximum without calculating eligibility or phase-outs; Massachusetts and Vermont child-credit helpers return zero; Missouri’s 2025 working-family credit is absent.
- **Retirement treatment is incomplete and often wrong.** Iowa’s full qualifying exclusion is missing, New York’s private-pension deduction is never applied, South Carolina’s under-65 calculation produces `NaN`, and numerous age, income, plan-type and spouse-specific restrictions are omitted.
- **Two federal assumptions require `useObbba2025` gates:** the hardcoded $2,200 federal child-credit base and the $40,000/$20,000 SALT cap. State-law changes effective in 2025 generally apply under both flag settings.
- All five scoped files were read completely. Imported rate data used by `stateOptimizer.js` was also examined. **No files were modified.** Findings were independently checked against primary sources; source discrepancies are identified below.

A CORRECT data row validates the stated amount/classification. Shared calculation defects are separately recorded and can still affect that state. “Missing” items are MISAPPLIED because default lookups return no benefit; MISSING-GATE is reserved for the policy flag.
